# Crispy Chicken Sandwich

## Assumptions
- All ingredients and cooking tools are available before starting.
- The chicken breast is thawed and ready to cook.
- The chicken breast is approximately 1 inch thick.
- The robot can measure ingredients and operate kitchen equipment.
- The robot can recognize when chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F.
- The robot will follow food-safety procedures when handling raw chicken.

## Ingredients
- 1 chicken breast
- 1 hamburger or brioche bun
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- Salt
- Black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Cooking oil
- Pickles
- Lettuce
- Mayonnaise

## Instructions
1. Place the chicken breast on a clean cutting surface.
2. Sprinkle 1/4 teaspoon salt onto one side of the chicken.
3. Sprinkle 1/4 teaspoon black pepper onto the same side.
4. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder onto the same side.
5. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon paprika onto the same side.
6. Turn the chicken breast over.
7. Repeat Steps 2–5 on the second side.
8. Place 1/2 cup flour into Bowl 1.
9. Crack 1 egg into Bowl 2.
10. Beat the egg until the yellow and white parts are combined.
11. Place 1/2 cup breadcrumbs into Bowl 3.
12. Place the chicken into Bowl 1.
13. Move the chicken through the flour until all surfaces are covered.
14. Remove the chicken from Bowl 1.
15. Place the chicken into Bowl 2.
16. Move the chicken through the egg until all surfaces are covered.
17. Remove the chicken from Bowl 2.
18. Place the chicken into Bowl 3.
19. Move the chicken through the breadcrumbs until all surfaces are covered.
20. Remove the chicken from Bowl 3.
21. Place a skillet on the stove.
22. Pour 2 tablespoons of cooking oil into the skillet.
23. Set the stove temperature to medium heat.
24. Wait until the oil is heated.
25. Place the coated chicken into the skillet.
26. Cook the chicken for 5 minutes.
27. Turn the chicken over.
28. Cook the chicken for another 5 minutes.
29. Measure the internal temperature of the chicken.
30. If the temperature is below 165°F, continue cooking.
31. Measure the temperature again after every 1 minute of additional cooking.
32. Stop cooking when the chicken reaches 165°F.
33. Remove the chicken from the skillet.
34. Place the cooked chicken on a clean plate.
35. Turn off the stove.
36. Cut the hamburger bun into two pieces.
37. Place both bun pieces in the skillet with the cut sides facing downward.
38. Toast the bun for 1 minute.
39. Remove the bun from the skillet.
40. Place the bottom bun on a clean plate.
41. Spread 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise across the top surface of the bottom bun.
42. Place 2 lettuce leaves on top of the mayonnaise.
43. Place the cooked chicken on top of the lettuce.
44. Place 4 pickle slices on top of the chicken.
45. Spread 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise across the bottom surface of the top bun.
46. Place the top bun on the pickles with the mayonnaise facing downward.
47. The chicken sandwich is complete.
